# Provenance and notice decision

This document is the current repository authority for provenance, licensing notices, and attribution. The owner approved public MIT distribution of the current package, retention of the Stan's Assets template notice, addition of `Copyright (c) 2026 Yurii Tor (Tor Production)`, and the minimal attribution in the package-root third-party notice.

The repository used the public Stan's Assets Unity Package Sample at commit `b59b0c9c7886d88f85b0449f338eabe9388d3831` as a Unity Package Manager repository template. The audited template is MIT-licensed and carries `Copyright (c) 2020 Stan's Assets`. The attribution applies only to template-derived material and does not identify Stan's Assets as an author or owner of the Tor Production Addressables implementation.

The released legal texts remain authoritative distribution artifacts:

- [MIT license](com.torproduction.addressables/LICENSE.md)
- [Third-party notices](com.torproduction.addressables/Third%20Party%20Notices.md)

Detailed engineering audit, owner-decision, history-sanitization, and release-execution records remain in the archived [ImplementationPlan.md](ImplementationPlan.md). Those historical records explain provenance but do not supersede this current decision. Private employment or contract narrative is intentionally excluded from the distributed UPM package.

Changing the license, copyright notices, attribution, or provenance conclusion requires separate explicit authorization and a new evidence-backed review.
